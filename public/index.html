Our Story

A Pinch of Passion,
A Cup of Magic

FlourWiz started in a tiny kitchen in 2020, born from a love for real, honest baking. No shortcuts, no artificial additives — just premium ingredients, time-honoured recipes and a whole lot of heart.

Our baker-in-chief, Pranita, believes every baked good should tell a story — of warmth, comfort and pure joy. From that little kitchen, we've grown into a beloved local bakery, but our soul remains the same.
